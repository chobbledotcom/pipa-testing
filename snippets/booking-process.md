      ## From enquiry to paperwork
    items:
      - icon: mdi:message-text-outline
        name: 1. Tell us what you have
        description: Send the equipment types, quantities, location and preferred date. Photos help when the correct test or a repair is unclear.
      - icon: mdi:clipboard-search-outline
        name: 2. Confirm the test route
        description: Dave checks which units are eligible for PIPA, which he may be competent to inspect in-house and which require another specialist before the visit is priced.
      - icon: mdi:calendar-check
        name: 3. Confirm the visit
        description: The office confirms the expected time, travel, likely cost and practical arrangements before reserving the slot.
      - icon: mdi:fan
        name: 4. Prepare the equipment
        description: Have the agreed units, blowers, paperwork and enough suitable space ready so Dave can work through the booking efficiently.
      - icon: mdi:clipboard-check-outline
        name: 5. Inspect and explain
        description: Dave carries out the work himself, records the findings and explains any faults or repairs that need attention.
      - icon: mdi:file-certificate-outline
        name: 6. Complete the paperwork
        description: Applicable PIPA records, tags and requested certificates are completed after each passing inspection.
