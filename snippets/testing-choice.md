      ## PIPA testing

      For inflatable play equipment within the current PIPA scheme. A passing inspection provides the applicable PIPA tag and database record, with a test certificate issued separately.

      **Usually relevant to:** mainstream inflatable play equipment such as bouncy castles, slides and obstacle courses.
