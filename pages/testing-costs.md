      ## Pricing questions
    items:
      - question: Is the PIPA online report included?
        answer: Yes. The applicable tag, database registration and online inspection report form part of the PIPA scheme record when an eligible unit passes. No separate certificate is issued for PIPA-scope equipment. The &pound;18 certificate applies only to eligible in-house inspections outside the scheme.
      - question: Is PAT part of the PIPA inspection?
        answer: No. PAT for agreed blowers is a separate electrical-maintenance service that Dave includes within his day rate. Extension-lead testing is charged separately.
      - question: How are travel costs worked out?
        answer: Travel and any necessary accommodation depend on the location and booking. The office will confirm them with the equipment list before the visit is agreed.
