      ## Dave carries out the inspection

      The office can answer your initial questions and arrange the booking. Dave personally turns up to do the inspection, with no subcontracting or handoff to a different inspector on the day.

Over the years he has inspected bouncy castles, slides, obstacle courses and other equipment within his qualifications. He can repair and reskin inflatable rodeo beds, but he does not inspect the mechanical mechanism. For inspection and ADIPS certification of the complete rodeo device, use an ADIPS-registered inspection body.

      ## From maintenance and repairs into inspection

      Dave's background in the industry began with maintenance and electrical work. He went on to repair inflatables and fans, moved into manufacturing and developed the hands-on knowledge that now informs his inspection work.

      That practical route connects the different parts of the service today: [PIPA testing](/pipa-testing/), [in-house inspections](/standard-test/), [inflatable repairs](/inflatable-repairs/) and [RPII operator courses](/rpii-courses/). It also means customers deal with somebody who understands how the equipment is built, used and maintained, not only how its paperwork is completed.
