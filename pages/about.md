      ## Dave carries out the inspection

      The office can answer your initial questions and arrange the booking. Dave personally turns up to do the inspection, with no subcontracting or handoff to a different inspector on the day.

      Dave is publicly listed by RPII under **Little Monsters** and works as part of the Monster Event Hire business. Over the years he has inspected bouncy castles, slides, obstacle courses and other equipment within his qualifications. He can repair and reskin inflatable rodeo beds, but he does not inspect the mechanical mechanism. For inspection and ADIPS certification of the complete rodeo device, use an ADIPS-registered inspection body.
