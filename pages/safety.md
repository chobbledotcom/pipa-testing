      ## Tagging and testing for bouncy castles and other inflatable play

      All inflatable play equipment that falls within the scope of PIPA should carry a PIPA tag and be listed in the database. **Don't risk your child's safety** — use only equipment with a current PIPA certificate.

      Non-PIPA-compliant inflatables can also be tested to BS EN 14960:2006.
