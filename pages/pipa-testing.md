      ## What is PIPA?

      PIPA, the **Professional Inflatable Play Association**, operates the UK's Inflatable Play Inspection Scheme. PIPA has a strong relationship with the Health and Safety Executive (HSE) and is part of the UK's Fairground Joint Advisory Committee. Its records give operators, venues and customers a straightforward way to confirm that eligible equipment has been independently inspected by a competent inspector.

      Equipment within scope includes eligible bouncy castles, disco domes, inflatable slides, obstacle courses, bouncing pillows, bungee runs and other inflatables designed for bouncing or sliding. Eligible constant-air non-ride-on games are also included.

      A unit that passes is PIPA tagged and recorded on the official PIPA database with its online inspection report. Dave carries out these inspections through **Little Monsters, a registered PIPA Inspection Body**, and is also an RPII-registered Annual Inflatable Inspector.

      ## What can be PIPA tested?

      The PIPA scheme covers specific types of commercial inflatable play equipment. Its scope develops over time, so Dave will confirm the correct inspection route for your particular unit before booking.
    items:

      - icon: mdi:run-fast
        name: Specialist play equipment
        description: Eligible bouncing pillows, disco domes and snowglobes are within scope. Bungee runs that meet BS EN 14960-4 are eligible, but devices made before Part 4 are not yet included.
      - icon: mdi:bullseye-arrow

      ## Non-ride-on games can now qualify

      PIPA now includes eligible constant-air games such as football shootouts and dartboards, basketball games, inflatable bowling alleys, mini golf cages, archery or axe games, inflatable four-in-a-row and side-stall games.

      Not every inflatable game qualifies. Dave may be able to inspect the inflatable parts of some ride-on or out-of-scope equipment and issue an in-house report after reviewing the device. Any such report covers only the inflatable component named in it; it does not certify an associated mechanism or complete amusement device.

      Dave can repair and reskin inflatable rodeo beds, but does not inspect the mechanical mechanism. For the complete rodeo device and ADIPS certification, use an ADIPS-registered inspection body.
