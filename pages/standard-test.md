      ## What will be tested

      - **Seams** and stitching
      - **Anchor points and accessories** — you will require 4 rows of stitching, the correct number of anchor stakes (18mm × 380mm) per inflatable
      - **Step size** — 1.5 times the bed height
      - **Markings** — including:
        1. Maximum number of users
        2. Maximum height of users (with correct measurement marking)
        3. Identification
        4. Blower / inspection tube marking
        5. Zips
      - **Internal air pressure test** — minimum of 4" on the manometer gauge
      - **Fall from height** — max 630mm
      - **Structural integrity**
      - **Sharp angles and edges**
      - **Fall-off height**
      - **Air-loss points**
      - **Entrapment**
      - **Deflation / evacuation time**
      - **Thread** — fire retardant
      - **Material** — fire retardant

      …and that's just to name a few.
