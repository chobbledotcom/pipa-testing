      ## Before the inspection
    items:
      - question: What should I send with my enquiry?
        answer: Send the equipment type, make or model where known, quantity, location and preferred date. A clear photograph helps Dave confirm the right test for unusual equipment.
      - question: Can Dave inspect equipment from any manufacturer?
        answer: The manufacturer alone does not decide whether Dave can inspect a unit. Send its make, model, photographs and available paperwork so he can confirm whether it falls within PIPA or whether he is competent to provide an in-house inspection. Some devices need a different specialist inspection body.
      - question: What needs to be ready on the day?
        answer: Have the agreed inflatables, their blowers and leads, manufacturer instructions, previous reports and tag details ready. Dave also needs enough suitable space to inflate and inspect each unit safely.
      - question: What does up to 20 units in a day mean?
        answer: The number depends on unit size, type, condition and how quickly each one can be set up and packed away. Having the equipment organised and help available for setup gives Dave more inspection time, but 20 is a maximum rather than a guarantee.
      - question: Can testing take place at my premises?
        answer: Yes. Testing at customer premises and travel across the UK are available for suitable bookings, or equipment can be brought to the Havant base by prior arrangement. The office will confirm whether the equipment list, journey and costs make the visit practical.
      - question: Can electrical equipment be tested at the same time?
        answer: PAT for agreed blowers is included in Dave's day rate as a separate electrical-maintenance service, not as part of the PIPA inspection. Extension leads are &pound;2.50 + VAT per reel when tested during the same visit.
      - question: What happens if a unit fails?
        answer: Dave explains the required work and can often complete minor repairs on-site. Every failure is different, so any repair and re-test cost is based on what the unit has failed on.
      - question: How can I check Dave's credentials?
        answer: Dave is listed by RPII as an Annual Inflatable Inspector under Little Monsters and serves on the PIPA Leadership Group. Links to both official records are on the [About Dave page](/about/).
